About Us

Tina Taylor, MN, ARNP, PMHNP-BC

Tina is a board-certified adult psychiatric nurse practitioner who has proudly served Cowlitz County for over 20 years, providing compassionate psychiatric medication management and counseling services. Known for her warmth, kindness, sense of humor, and easygoing communication style, she creates a supportive environment where clients feel heard, respected, and genuinely cared for.

Read moreRead less

Tina is passionate about treating the whole person and believes in a client-centered, strength-based approach to care. She values each client’s voice and works collaboratively with other professionals to provide thoughtful, personalized treatment. Using evidence-based practices, she supports adults experiencing depression, anxiety, PTSD, and trauma-related concerns. Her treatment approach may include psychiatric medication, counseling, and integrative strategies such as nutrition, exercise, stress management, and other natural supports.

In addition to medication management, Tina incorporates counseling into most follow-up appointments, drawing from supportive therapy, interpersonal therapy, solution-focused therapy, cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT), EMDR, and mindfulness-based techniques. Her extensive clinical experience, strong assessment skills, and intuitive, compassionate approach allow her to develop individualized treatment plans that reflect each client’s unique needs and goals.

Tina earned her Bachelor of Science in Nursing in 1990 and completed her Master of Nursing in 2004, becoming board certified as an adult psychiatric nurse practitioner. Her background includes experience in medical, cardiac, home care, inpatient, outpatient, and psychiatric settings. She also has extensive experience serving the indigenous Native American community, further enriching her culturally informed and community-centered care.

Leslie Stanyer, LMHC, SUDP, Certified Gambling Counselor

Leslie Stanyer is a Licensed Mental Health Counselor dedicated to supporting individuals and families as they work toward healing, stability, and personal growth. She brings a strong foundation in social work, ethical practice, and compassionate client-centered care to her work with clients facing a wide range of life and mental health challenges.

Read moreRead less

Leslie earned her Master of Social Work degree from Arizona State University, where she developed a broad understanding of social work principles, evidence-based treatment, advocacy, and ethical clinical practice. Her education and professional experience have equipped her to support clients with concerns including anxiety, depression, PTSD, trauma-related symptoms, substance use, and gambling-related challenges.

Leslie’s approach is collaborative, supportive, and tailored to each client’s unique needs, strengths, and goals. She values empathy, active listening, and practical skill-building while helping clients feel heard, respected, and empowered. Whether working with individuals or families, Leslie focuses on creating a safe and welcoming therapeutic environment where clients can explore challenges, build insight, and develop tools for meaningful change.

In addition to being a Washington State Licensed Mental Health Counselor, Leslie also holds a Substance Use Disorder Professional credential through Washington State and is a Certified Gambling Counselor through the Evergreen Council on Problem Gambling. She remains committed to ongoing professional development and strives to provide ethical, informed, and high-quality care.

Leslie looks forward to partnering with clients on their path toward wellness, healing, and greater fulfillment.

Amanda Baker, MSW, LMHCA, Certified Counselor

Amanda Baker is a compassionate and dedicated therapist who holds a Master of Social Work with clinical concentration from Portland State University. As a Licensed Mental Health Counselor Associate and Certified Counselor, she brings extensive experience working with children, adolescents, adults, and families facing a wide range of emotional, behavioral, and life challenges.

Read moreRead less

Drawing from her background in family therapy and crisis intervention, Amanda provides trauma-informed care that is grounded in empathy, collaboration, and respect. She understands that seeking support can feel overwhelming, and she strives to create a welcoming, nonjudgmental environment where clients feel safe, heard, and valued from the very first session.

Amanda has a particular passion for helping children and families navigate challenging behaviors, emotional regulation difficulties, relationship concerns, life transitions, and the effects of trauma. She works closely with clients to identify their strengths, build practical coping skills, and develop healthier patterns of communication and connection. Her warm, approachable style helps clients feel comfortable expressing difficult emotions while fostering trust, resilience, and personal growth.

Amanda believes that meaningful change happens when clients feel genuinely understood and supported. Her goal is to provide a therapeutic space where individuals and families can build confidence, strengthen relationships, overcome obstacles, and move toward a healthier, more fulfilling life. Through compassion, authenticity, and evidence-informed care, Amanda empowers clients to discover their strengths and create lasting positive change.
